# Rushed Crops

WASD move, mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump, E interact, 1-5 hotbar, left click use tool, Esc release mouse.

You start empty-handed. Pick up the hoe and watering can from the stump by the plots, and the hatchet from the chopping block. With a tool in hand, E tills, plants, waters and harvests. Refill the can at the river.

Sell crops to the merchant at the stall and buy seeds, wood and stone. Then repair the barn: 40 coins to unlock it, 20 coins and 4 wood for the roof, 25 coins with 3 wood and 4 stone for the east wall once the carrot boss east of the field is beaten. A day lasts eight minutes.
