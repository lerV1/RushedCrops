# Rushed Crops

WASD move, mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump. E interacts, 1–9 or the mouse wheel selects a hotbar slot, and left click uses the held tool. H eats food when injured. Backspace drops one selected item; Shift + Backspace drops its stack. Escape or E closes the merchant window. F5 saves.

You start empty-handed. Look at the hoe and watering can near the stump, or the hatchet on the chopping block, and press E to pick them up. Select the hoe and left click clear soil inside the crop field to till it. Hold seeds and press E on the tilled square to plant. Select the watering can and left click the river to refill it, then left click the planted square to water it. A carrot takes 45 seconds of watered growth and yields three carrots when harvested with E. Some other crops need a second watering.

Use the hatchet on trees for wood and the pickaxe on quarry rocks for stone. The pickaxe lies at the quarry entrance. Collect the loose drops. Sell crops, wood, and stone to the merchant at the stall, then buy seeds or replacement supplies. Prices change each day. A day lasts eight minutes.

The first boss tube is available from the start. Barn repairs unlock the other three tubes. The entrance costs 600 coins. The roof costs 1,050 coins and 30 wood. The walls cost 2,050 coins, 30 wood, and 20 stone, and require at least one defeated boss. Costs are paid together; an unaffordable job spends nothing.

Evil mini crops climb out of the crop field now and then. Each has a 5% chance to drop its boss seed; plant and water it twice and its boss climbs out. Beat the Carrot King, Tomato Brute and Onion Overlord (each counts once) and finish the barn to win. The Beet Baron, Cabbage Colossus and Wheat Warlord are bonus fights.

The sword and capture jar are near the market stall. A jar captures a living boss only at one-third health or less. Leave room in the hotbar for the capture, then press E on an unlocked tube to place it inside. It produces seeds over time; press E again to collect them when there is room. Capturing a boss and killing it are different outcomes: the three required boss defeats still count toward victory.

Use Play on the title screen to continue a saved farm. New Game asks before replacing existing progress. Death's Restart button returns you to your checkpoint with full health. Completing the barn and defeating all three required bosses displays the Farm Restored banner.

The existing developer menu opens with C while playing the farm. It supplies coins, items, healing, and water for demonstration setup. C or Escape closes it and restores gameplay.
