# Rushed Crops

A short first-person farming game. You start with nothing, grow crops on the farm, sell them at the market, and repair the old barn.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D or arrow keys | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Space | Jump |
| E | Use what you are looking at |
| 1 to 5 | Choose a hotbar slot |
| Left click | Use the item in your hand |
| Backspace | Drop one of the held item |
| Shift + Backspace | Drop the whole stack |
| Esc | Release the mouse |

Gamepads work too.

## Picking up your tools

You begin with empty pockets. The hoe and the watering can are on a tree stump beside the farm plots. The hatchet lies on a chopping block next to the straw practice dummy. Look at a tool and press E.

Tools take one hotbar slot each and do not stack. Press 1 to 5 to switch between them.

## Farming

Till a square by looking at it with the hoe and pressing E. Choose seeds in your hotbar and press E again to plant them. Fill the watering can at the river, then press E on the square to water it.

Crops grow through three stages. Harvest a ripe crop with E.

| Crop | Time | Waterings | Harvest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beet | 45s | 1 | 2 |
| Carrot | 60s | 1 | 2 |
| Onion | 75s | 1 | 3 |
| Tomato | 90s | 2 | 3 |
| Wheat | 100s | 2 | 4 |
| Cabbage | 120s | 2 | 2 |

Some crops need a second watering before they finish. A prompt on screen tells you what E will do.

## The merchant

The merchant waits at the market stall. Press E on him to open his shop. He sells seeds, wood and stone, and buys the crops you bring him. His prices shift every day.

## The barn

The barn beside the field has three jobs. Look at it and press E to pay for the next one.

| Job | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock the barn | 40 coins |
| Patch the roof | 20 coins, 4 wood |
| Straighten the east wall | 25 coins, 3 wood, 4 stone |

The last job waits until the boss is beaten.

## The boss

A giant carrot stands east of the crop field. Hit it with the hatchet. It takes three hits, and it drops seeds for every crop when it falls.

## Health

A health bar shows how much health you have left. If it runs out you wake at the last checkpoint with full health. Checkpoints are at the farm and by the market.

## Time

A day lasts eight minutes, and the sun follows it.

## In the browser

Click Play to start. The game captures the mouse; press Esc to let go of it.
