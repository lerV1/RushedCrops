# Rushed Crops

WASD move, mouse look, Shift sprint, Space jump, E interact, 1-5 hotbar, left click use tool, Esc release mouse.

You start empty-handed. Pick up the hoe and watering can from the stump by the plots, and the hatchet from the chopping block. With a tool in hand, E tills, plants, waters and harvests. Refill the can at the river.

Chop the oaks round the logging camp for wood and mine the quarry rocks for stone (the pickaxe lies at the quarry entrance). Sell crops, wood and stone to the merchant at the stall, and buy seeds there, or wood and stone if you run short. Then repair the barn: 40 coins to unlock it, 20 coins and 4 wood for the roof, 25 coins with 3 wood and 4 stone for the east wall once any boss is beaten. A day lasts eight minutes.

Evil mini crops climb out of the crop field now and then. Each has a 5% chance to drop its boss seed; plant and water it twice and its boss climbs out. Beat the Carrot King, Tomato Brute and Onion Overlord (each counts once) and finish the barn to win. The Beet Baron, Cabbage Colossus and Wheat Warlord are bonus fights.
